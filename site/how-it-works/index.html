The rules of the floor

How it works

BAWSAQ is a player-run exchange for Vice City. You buy shares with Solana, the price moves on every trade, and you sell to whoever shows up next. Six steps, no accounts, no waiting rooms.

SOL —Fee 5%Treasury 3tZb…SqRr
01

Bring a wallet

No email, no password, no signup form. You connect Phantom or Solflare and that wallet is your account. Same wallet, same holdings, every time you come back.

Phantom · Solflare
02

Pick something in Vice City

Four boards: Stocks, Companies, Real Estate and Rides. Every listing shows a picture, the live price, a price chart, who owns what and how much of the supply is gone.

Stocks · Companies · Real Estate · Rides
03

Buy shares with SOL

Everyone can invest — nothing is locked to a single owner. You pick a share count, approve one payment in your wallet, and the shares land in your portfolio the moment Solana confirms the transaction.

Pays in SOL · Shown in dollars
04

The price moves with you

Every share bought from the exchange pushes that asset up 1.5%. The chart is drawn from real trades, not a simulation — the more people pile in, the higher it climbs.

+1.5% per share bought
05

Sell to another player

Already holding? List your shares at any price you like. Your wallet signs the listing so nobody else can post it for you, and you can pull it whenever you want.

You set the price
06

Get paid, take your cut

A buyer pays you and the exchange in one transaction: you get 95%, the exchange keeps 5%. The shares move to the buyer and the trade is recorded on both your pages.

95% seller · 5% exchange fee
